Privacy Policy — Power Ascendant

Last updated: August 9, 2026 · Contact: admin@pascgame.net

This policy explains what information the Power Ascendant mobile game ("the game", "we") does and does not collect. In short: the game runs on your device, stores your progress locally, and has no servers or accounts. The only data processing happens when you make the optional in-app purchase, which is handled by Google Play and RevenueCat.

Information we collect

We do not collect personal information. The game has no user accounts, no login, and no analytics. Your game progress (power, gear, upgrades, settings, etc.) is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted to us or anyone else.

Purchases

The game is free. It offers one optional one-time purchase, the Supporter Pack. Purchases are processed by Google Play Billing and managed via RevenueCat. To deliver and restore your purchase, these services process a purchase token and a pseudonymous, app-generated identifier. We never receive your name, email address, or payment details. Their handling of that data is governed by their own privacy policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • RevenueCat Privacy Policy

Advertising & third-party sharing

The game contains no ads. We do not sell or share your data with third parties for advertising or any other purpose.

Children

The game is suitable for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data retention & deletion

Because your progress is stored only on your device, you can delete it at any time by using the in-game reset/wipe option or by uninstalling the app. Purchase records are retained by Google and RevenueCat according to their policies.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy; the "Last updated" date above will change accordingly.
